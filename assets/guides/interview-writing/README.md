# Fictional interview: actual app output

`source-script.txt` is an original fictional bakery-owner interview written by Texter's operator. It contains no real interviewee's personal information. `sample-interview.m4a` is a synthetic reading, not a recording of a real interview.

The audio was generated on 2026-11-11 with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), its [official inference library](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro), and the Japanese `jf_alpha` voice. The model and library are published under Apache-2.0. Sentences were split onto separate lines for synthesis without changing their text. WAV output was converted to mono AAC in M4A, with source metadata removed. Duration: approximately 186 seconds.

The audio was imported into Texter development version 2.11.73 on an iPhone Simulator running iOS 26.5. Whisper (OpenAI) was selected; line breaks were on, translation was off, and speaker identification was not used. `transcript.txt` and `summary.txt` were copied from that note without editing. The two PNGs show the same processed note. The guide separately identifies human corrections and editorial examples.

This is one synthetic, single-speaker example. It does not establish accuracy or processing speed for real interviews, background noise, multiple speakers, other devices, or other versions. Processing time was not measured.
