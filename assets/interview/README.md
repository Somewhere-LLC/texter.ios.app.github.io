# Fictional interview audio

The Japanese and English `first`, `second`, and `third` clips are synthetic readings of the adjacent `.txt` scripts. The people, interviews, dates, and project in the landing-page demo are fictional. The demo's questions and answers are fixed examples.

Audio regenerated on 2026-11-11 using [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) and its [official inference library](https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro), published under Apache-2.0. Japanese uses `jf_alpha`; English uses `am_michael`. No individual's voice was supplied or cloned for these clips.

The WAV output was converted to mono AAC in an M4A container. These clips illustrate source playback; they are not evidence of Texter's transcription accuracy, processing speed, or AI responses.
