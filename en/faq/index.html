Getting started

What is Texter?

Texter is an AI transcription and note-taking app that turns meetings, interviews, and lectures into notes. Record or import an audio or video file, and Texter transcribes it, labels speakers, summarizes it, and extracts action items. You can ask questions about your saved notes later with AI chat.

Which devices does Texter support?

  • iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS 16.0 or later)
  • Apple Watch (watchOS 11 or later)
  • Mac with Apple silicon (runs as the iPad app)
  • Android (on Google Play)

What do the tabs at the bottom of the screen do?

  • List: browse and search notes, manage folders
  • Voice: live transcription, and translation from the translate icon at the top right
  • Image: transcribe images, videos, and PDFs
  • Settings: file import, voice input and transcription, text and display, app settings, help

Which settings should I check first?

If you use technical terms or company names, register them under Settings → Voice Input & Transcription → Word Registration (Technical Terms, etc.). For translation, choose the language and service in Translation Settings on the same screen.

Can I start recording without opening the app?

Yes. Start recording with one tap from the Lock Screen widget. On iOS 18 or later you can also add Texter to Control Center.

Recording and live transcription

How do I transcribe a meeting live?

On the Voice tab, tap the microphone, speak, then stop. Depending on the language and your settings, Texter uses a cloud speech engine built for conversation or Apple speech recognition. To save automatically, turn on Save Real-Time Transcription Results under Settings → Voice Input & Transcription. The free plan allows 3 minutes per session and 15 minutes a day.

How can I improve accuracy?

Speak close to the microphone and reduce background noise. Add names and terms in Word Registration (Technical Terms, etc.), with the word and its reading separated by a space. Audio Recording Settings also has a noise reduction option.

Can Texter clean up punctuation and paragraphs?

Turn on AI Text Formatting in Settings. Texter then adds punctuation and paragraphs after recording. This option may not appear.

Can I keep transcribing while I use another app?

Yes. Switch the live transcription screen to Picture in Picture, and it continues when you change apps. The free plan time limit still applies.

Can I use voice input in other apps?

Yes. Add the Texter keyboard in iPhone Settings and turn on Allow Full Access. It does not work in password fields. The free plan allows 3 minutes a day.

Can I record on Apple Watch?

Yes. Apple Watch records on its own, without your iPhone nearby. The recording transfers to your iPhone later and is transcribed there. Each recording can be up to 15 minutes on the free plan and up to 2 hours with Premium. Transcription still stops when the free plan allowance runs out.

Does Texter work offline?

Some features do. Without a network connection, live transcription switches to on-device Apple speech recognition (in languages that support it). On iOS 26 or later you can transcribe files with SpeechAnalyzer, which runs on the device. Translation can use Google’s offline mode. AI features such as summaries and AI chat need an internet connection.

Transcribing files

Can I transcribe audio or video files I already have?

Yes. Open Settings → Import Files, then choose Import Audio or Import Video. For a video, choose whether to keep the video or extract the audio only. Supported formats:

  • Audio: m4a, mp3, wav, aac, aiff, caf, and more
  • Video: mp4, mov, m4v

How do I choose the transcription service?

Go to Settings → Import Files → Choose Your Preferred Speech Recognition Service.

  • Default: uses Apple speech recognition
  • SpeechAnalyzer: runs on the device (iOS 26 or later)
  • Whisper(OpenAI): runs on a server
  • Amazon Transcribe: runs on a server (Premium)

My audio is transcribed in the wrong language.

Go to Settings → Import Files → Choose Your Preferred Speech Recognition Service, set the Whisper Recognition Language, and import again. If the problem continues, contact us and tell us about the audio quality and how you imported it.

Can I import from a cloud share link?

Yes. Use Settings → Import Files → Import from link for Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive share links. OneDrive requires signing in with a Microsoft account. Zoom recording links cannot be imported directly, so download the recording first. URLs from video services such as YouTube are not supported.

What is silence removal?

This Premium feature removes silent parts when you import a file. Set it with Auto Remove Silence under Settings → Import Files. Cut Strength can be Weak, Standard, or Strong.

I get an error when I import a file. What can I do at the limit?

The file may be over the limit. With Premium, the per-file limits are 1 GB and 2 hours for Whisper, or 2 GB and 4 hours for Amazon Transcribe. Amazon Transcribe also has a monthly allowance that depends on your plan. The free plan has a monthly limit. Try splitting the file or converting it to a lighter format. When the free allowance runs out, the import limit screen offers Premium or an import ticket. Tickets are for Whisper transcription only and work for audio up to 2 hours.

Speakers, languages, and translation

Can Texter tell speakers apart?

Yes, with Premium. When you transcribe an audio or video file with Whisper or Amazon Transcribe, Texter separates the text by speaker. Whisper can detect the number of speakers or use a number you set, and Amazon Transcribe distinguishes up to 10 speakers. Live transcription also offers speaker labels as a beta feature.

Which languages are supported?

File transcription with Whisper supports 99 languages plus automatic language detection. Languages for live transcription depend on the speech engine in use.

Can I translate what I say?

Yes, in two ways.

  • Translation screen: tap the translate icon at the top right of the Voice tab. Choose DeepL, Google online, or Google offline, and change the service under Settings → Voice Input & Transcription → Translation Settings
  • Live Translation: turn on the translate button to the right of the microphone during recording, and translations appear with the transcript. Choose the target language on the recording screen. This uses Apple’s translation feature, so it requires iOS 18 or later

Images and PDFs

How do I extract text from an image or PDF?

On the Image tab, choose Choose from Camera or Photo Library, Import Image Files (PDF included), or Select from Screenshots. Keep the result in a note with the save button.

Text in my image is not read correctly.

Retake the photo in good light without tilt or shadows. Choose the language under Settings → Voice Input & Transcription → Image-to-Text Recognition Languages. For a PDF, use a high-resolution original.

Summaries, tasks, and AI chat

Can Texter write meeting minutes and summaries?

Yes. There are 20 templates, such as meeting minutes, interview, lecture, and structured summary. To summarize automatically after recording, set Auto Summary under Settings → Text & Display → Summary & Text Conversion Settings.

Can Texter pull out action items from a meeting?

Yes. AI finds the to-dos in the transcript, along with who owns each one and when it is due. Each task can become a reminder in one tap, and Texter notifies you at the time you set.

Can I ask questions about my past recordings?

Yes. Tap the chat icon at the bottom right of a note and ask things like “What deadline did we agree on?” The note list also lets you ask across several notes. The free plan allows 3 questions a day, and Premium has no daily limit.

Where do I open Topic Overview?

Open the menu at the top right of a note, then choose Topic Overview under Visualization. It groups a long note by topic. Word Cloud is in the same menu.

Can Texter name notes automatically?

Yes. On iOS 26 or later, on devices where the on-device AI model is available, a title is added after recording.

Managing and sharing notes

Can I organize notes in folders?

Create folders in the Folder view of the List tab, and use Move to Folder for a note. Sort by created date, last updated, or title.

How do I share or export a note?

Open the menu at the top right of a note, then use Export.

  • Notes: text, PDF, SRT subtitle file, video with captions
  • Structured summaries: Markdown, text, HTML, PDF

SRT is available only for notes with subtitle data. Create Public Link shares the note with a URL.

How do I post to Slack?

Enter your webhook URL under Settings → App Settings → Slack Integration Settings, then send a test message.

How do I set a reminder?

Tap the bell icon at the top of a note. Repeat options include once, daily, weekly, and every month, and More repeat options... offers shorter intervals. Snooze can be 1 or 5 minutes.

Where do I change text size and alignment?

Go to Settings → Text & Display → Text • Highlight • Subtitles • Split.

Can I hide video subtitles?

Turn off Show Subtitles under Settings → Text & Display → Text • Highlight • Subtitles • Split.

Pricing, Premium, and billing

Is Texter free? How much is Premium?

The app is free to download, and the basic features are free to use. The free plan limits transcription time and how often you can use each feature.

Texter Premium raises those limits and comes in weekly, monthly, and yearly plans. Prices vary by country and are shown in your local currency on the App Store and in the app. Check the purchase screen in the app for any free trial and its length.

What are the main limits on the free plan?

  • Real-time transcription: 3 minutes per session, 15 minutes a day
  • File transcription with Whisper: a monthly limit applies
  • Summaries: 3 a day
  • AI chat: 3 a day
  • Image transcription: 5 a day
  • Word cloud: 5 a day

Amazon Transcribe is available with Premium. Limits can change, so check the plan comparison in the app for the latest details.

What changes with Premium?

Premium raises the time and usage limits and removes ads. It also adds speaker labels, Amazon Transcribe, and silence removal. Check the plan comparison in the app for what each plan includes.

Can I buy extra usage?

Premium members can use Add More Usage. Free users may see an import ticket for one hour of Whisper transcription on the import limit screen. Tickets do not renew automatically, and unused time does not expire. They do not cover live recording, summaries, AI chat, or translation.

Premium is not active after I changed phones.

Sign in with the Apple ID you used to buy, then try Restore Purchases in the app. Also check your App Store purchase history.

What about billing and refunds?

App Store charges, receipts, and refunds follow Apple’s rules. Contact Apple Support about an unintended charge. A free trial starts charging automatically when it ends.

How do I cancel Premium?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Texter. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Can my company use Texter?

Yes. Our business brochure (PDF) covers features, pricing, and security. For questions, email texter.work.app@gmail.com.

Privacy and your data

Where are my recordings and notes stored?

Recordings and notes are stored on your device. Texter has no sync feature that keeps your notes on our servers. Deleting the app also removes your notes, so export anything you need before you switch phones or delete the app.

Is my audio or text ever sent outside the device?

When you use a feature that runs in the cloud, Texter sends the audio or text it needs to the service provider. The main features are live transcription (including Apple speech recognition), the voice keyboard, file transcription with Whisper or Amazon Transcribe, translation, summaries, structured summaries, task extraction, AI chat, Topic Overview, AI Text Formatting, Slack posts, and public links. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Are notes I use with summaries or AI chat used to train AI models?

Summaries, structured summaries, task extraction, and AI chat send the note text to the OpenAI API to generate results. Under the OpenAI API data usage policy, that data is not used to train OpenAI’s models.

Troubleshooting and contact

A permission error stops me from recording.

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and allow Texter. Allow Speech Recognition in the same way.

How do I contact support?

Go to Settings → Help & Support → Contact Us. It helps to include the feature name, the steps to reproduce the problem, and your device and OS version. You can also email texter.work.app@gmail.com.
