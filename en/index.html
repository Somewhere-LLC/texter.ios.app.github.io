1. Record

Record on your phone,
from files, or from your wrist.

Start capturing the moment you think of it.

🎙️

Record and transcribe live

One tap starts recording and transcribes as you speak. Long meetings and lectures are captured as they happen.

📂

Import audio and video

Bring in voice memos, meeting-tool recordings, or video files for transcription. Keep past recordings in one place.

⌚

Apple Watch and widgets

Record on Apple Watch alone and hand it off to iPhone. Start instantly from the Lock Screen widget, the Side button (iOS 26.2+), or Siri and Shortcuts.

2. Organize

From transcript to summary,
automatically.

By the time you stop recording, it already reads like notes.

📝

Accurate transcription with speaker labels

Powered by the latest engines including Whisper (OpenAI). Punctuation is added automatically and each speaker's lines are kept apart.

🧠

Summaries and Topic Summary

20 templates for meetings, interviews, lectures and more. Topic Summary picks the right structure for the content, from two-column comparisons to flowcharts.

🌐

100+ languages and translation

Transcribe in more than 100 languages, and translate on the spot with DeepL or Google Translate.

3. Recall

Ask your past recordings
a question.

Ask Your Note works on a single note or across All Notes. Every answer comes with sources, and you can play the original words right there.

  • 💬

    Ask across All Notes

    "What deadline did we agree on last month?" Ask across your notes instead of searching through them.

  • 📎

    Answers with sources

    Each answer shows which note and which line it came from. No unsupported claims.

  • ▶️

    Play the original words

    Tap "Play from 12:34" on a source card to hear that exact moment again. Check the record, not your memory.

4. Act

Your meeting becomes
a to-do list

AI pulls out what needs doing. No more copying tasks out of your notes.

🎯

Automatic extraction

"Can you handle this?" "By next week" — AI catches tasks buried in conversation and turns them into a clean list.

⏰

One-tap Reminders

Due dates and owners are detected and sent straight to Apple Reminders, so notifications just work.

📤

Share and export

Export transcripts, summaries and action items as text, PDF or Markdown, and share them with your team on the spot.
